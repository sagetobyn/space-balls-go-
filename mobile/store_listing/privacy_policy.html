Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 8, 2025

App: Space Balls GO!

Developer: Hassium Labs

Summary: Space Balls GO! is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect personal information. Game progress is stored locally on your device only.

1. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Contacts or photos

Data stored locally on your device:

  • Game progress (completed levels, stars earned)
  • Settings preferences (sound, music, vibration)
  • Purchase status (ads removed)

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

Google AdMob (Advertisements)

We display ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address
  • General device information

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Services

For in-app purchases, we use Google Play Billing. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

Space Balls GO! is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation or personal data submission.

Parents can disable personalized ads in their device settings if desired.

4. Data Retention

Since we don't collect personal data, there is no data to retain on our servers. Your game progress is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by:

  • Using the "Erase All Data" option in Settings
  • Clearing app data in your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your local game data at any time
  • Opt out of personalized ads via device settings
  • Remove ads via in-app purchase
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

6. Data Security

Your game data is stored locally using secure device storage. We do not transmit or store your data on external servers.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
